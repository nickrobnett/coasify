01 — About
A holding company for frictionless financial infrastructure.

Coasify Corporation is the legal and institutional parent behind a portfolio of DeFi protocols. Its name derives from the Coase theorem: when transaction costs approach zero, resources naturally flow to their most efficient allocation.

The green-and-gold palette is not incidental. It carries decades of institutional precedent — British racing, Ivy League, old-money asset management — and redeployed here it signals exactly the right register: permanent, considered, not trying to impress anyone.

★ Hunter (#2D5A3D) and Antique Gold (#B08A2A) are the two brand-defining colors. The gold is deliberately desaturated — it reads as aged and permanent rather than flashy or festive. If it looks like a sports trophy, it's too bright. If it looks like a brass doorknob on a law firm's entrance, it's right.

Why gold works here

Gold is the warm complement to cool green — they sit on opposite sides of the color wheel. This is the same logic that made teal work with brown, but the cultural resonance of green-and-gold is deeper and more institutional. It has a settled, earned quality that teal doesn't.

What gold signals

Permanence. Standard-setting. Quality that doesn't need to announce itself. Used sparingly — pull-quote rules, active states, divider accents, the occasional data callout — it elevates without dominating. It should feel discovered, not plastered.

What to avoid

Gold on white backgrounds reads as yellow. Use it on green or cream surfaces where the warm/cool contrast lands properly. And keep the saturation muted — #B08A2A, not #FFD700. Bright gold is celebration; antique gold is institution.

Merch case

Hunter green + antique gold is a nearly perfect t-shirt palette. The wordmark in Parchment Gold (#E8D4A0) on Hunter reads as premium without effort. The inverse — cream tee, Hunter green text with a gold rule — is also strong. No workarounds needed.

08 — Voice & Tone
Do

State the mechanism plainly. "Zero-fee 1:1 swaps between stablecoins."

Use economic language that rewards the reader for knowing it.

One idea per sentence. Short paragraphs. No exclamation points.

Don't

Lead with disruption. "We're revolutionizing the stablecoin ecosystem."

Use crypto slang in Coasify materials.

Explain what the Coase theorem is — the audience knows.

09 — Usage rules
RuleRationale
Light backgrounds only.Dollar Store owns dark. Distinction is brand value.
Gold appears no more than 2–3 times per page.Scarcity is what makes it feel like gold, not decoration.
Never use bright gold (#FFD700 range). Stay at or below #C9A84C.Bright gold is festive. Antique gold is institutional.
No gradients, no shadows, no blur.Flat surfaces communicate confidence.
No rounded corners beyond 3px.Square-ish corners read institutional, not consumer.
No monospace typography.Monospace is Dollar Store's voice.
Max one primary CTA per view.Institutions don't shout. One clear next step.
Sentence case everywhere.Caps shout. Sentence case is how settled institutions write.
