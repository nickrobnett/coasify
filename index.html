The thesis

When transaction costs approach zero, resources flow to their most efficient allocation.

The name comes from the Coase theorem, and the theorem is the business plan. Every basis point of friction in a financial system is a quiet tax on coordination. Remove the friction and markets organize themselves.

Coasify exists to identify the places where that friction is pure artifact — cost without compensating function — and to build the infrastructure that eliminates it.

“When transaction costs approach zero, resources flow to their most efficient allocation.”

Ronald Coase — The Problem of Social Cost, 1960
First protocol

Dollar Store

DeFi protocol Stablecoins

Dollar Store enables zero-fee, frictionless 1:1 swaps between tokenized representations of the same underlying asset.

The thesis is simple: stablecoins are a coordination problem, not a price discovery problem. A dollar is a dollar. Charging a spread to move between two representations of the same dollar is friction without function — exactly the kind of cost the protocol exists to remove.
